# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to combine PDFs and images into a clean final document without learning a complex editor. They work in a focused desktop workflow where source files may be private, documents may be long, and the final page order must remain predictable.

## Product Purpose

Fyler turns mixed local files into one PDF through a clear sequence: add sources, choose pages, reorder the result, preview it, and export. Success means users can assemble the intended document quickly, verify it with confidence, and keep source files unchanged.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, dependable. The interface should feel capable without becoming technical or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense professional-editor chrome, playful effects that obscure document state, hidden gestures without visible alternatives, and decorative interfaces that compete with page previews.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the document state obvious at every step.
2. Prefer direct manipulation with visible, accessible alternatives.
3. Make common actions immediate and advanced options progressive.
4. Preserve user confidence through previews, reversible interaction, and non-destructive edits.
5. Keep performance predictable for both short and long documents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for product controls. Preserve keyboard-operable alternatives for pointer interactions, clear focus states, screen-reader labels and status announcements, adequate contrast across themes, and reduced-motion preferences.
